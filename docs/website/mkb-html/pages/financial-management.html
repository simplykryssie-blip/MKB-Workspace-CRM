Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can I purchase payroll without bookkeeping?

Yes. Payroll is available on its own. Payroll processing is priced by the number of workers, starting at $175/month, and payroll tax compliance can be added as a separate service.

Can I add payroll to my bookkeeping package later?

Yes. You can start with bookkeeping and add payroll whenever you’re ready. We’ll scope it with you and add it to your service agreement at our standard payroll pricing.

Can I purchase bookkeeping without payroll?

Yes. Bookkeeping packages start at $399/month and don’t require payroll.

How does monthly bookkeeping work?

Each month we record and categorize your transactions, reconcile your accounts, and close the month. Monthly completion is within 10 days, and your Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow statements are delivered by the 5th day after that 10-day completion period.

What happens if my books are behind?

Catch-up and cleanup are available as separate, assessment-based services. We review where your books stand and scope the work before monthly bookkeeping begins.

What payroll information is required to get started?

After you sign on, we collect what we need — such as your business details, pay schedule, worker information and pay rates, prior payroll reports if you’re switching providers, and your payroll tax account details if compliance is included. All of it is collected securely through your client portal, never through a public website form.

Do you handle payroll tax filings?

Payroll tax compliance is a separate service from payroll processing. Its base scope covers federal and one state, priced by the size of your team; additional states are scoped separately. Your service agreement lists exactly what is included.

Can MKB support multiple business entities?

Yes. Additional entity or location support starts at $150/month, depending on complexity, and each entity’s records are kept separate.

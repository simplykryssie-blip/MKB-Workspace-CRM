Important

What we do — and what we don’t

MKB Financial Group provides administrative assistance and document preparation. MKB is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation.

We do not decide whether you are eligible for an expungement, and completing our intake does not mean you are eligible. Courts and agencies make their own determinations, and we cannot promise an outcome or a timeline.

For legal advice about your situation, please consult a licensed attorney.

Questions

Common questions.

Can you tell me if I’m eligible?

No. We can’t determine eligibility or give legal advice. Eligibility is decided under the law that applies to your record, by the court or agency involved. For advice about your situation, consult a licensed attorney.

Do you guarantee my record will be expunged?

No. Courts and agencies make their own decisions, and we cannot promise any outcome.

How long does it take?

It depends on the agencies and courts involved, including how long records requests take. We can’t promise a timeframe, but you can follow your progress in your client portal.

Are you a law firm?

No. MKB Financial Group is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation.

## Resources

**H1:** Understand your business beyond the bank balance.
Practical information for business owners who want to understand their books, taxes, payroll,
financial responsibilities, and the decisions that come with running a business.

**START HERE — Resources built around the questions business owners actually ask.**
01 Bookkeeping — Know your numbers. Learn how to read your P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow,
reconcile accounts, organize receipts, and understand what your books are telling you.
02 Tax — Prepare and plan. Understand tax documents, preparation, estimated-tax concepts,
deductions, and why tax planning should happen before the filing deadline.
03 Payroll — Keep payroll organized. Learn the difference between payroll processing, payroll tax
compliance, and payroll bookkeeping—and why each matters.
04 Business — Build the foundation. Business setup, financial systems, banking, QuickBooks
organization, and the administrative practices that support growth.

**BOOKKEEPING BASICS — Your books are more than a record of what already happened.** Use
financial statements to understand revenue, expenses, profitability, cash position, and trends.
Good bookkeeping gives you organized information to work from.
01 Profit & Loss — understand income and expenses. 02 Balance Sheet — understand what the
business owns and owes. 03 Cash Flow — understand how money moves. 04 Reconciliations — make
sure records agree with financial accounts.

**TAX & COMPLIANCE — Tax season should not be the first time you look at your numbers.**
Organized records make tax preparation easier to support. Planning is different from
preparation: preparation reports what happened; planning considers future decisions and their
potential tax effects. For specific tax advice, filing requirements, deadlines, or current law,
work with your tax professional based on your circumstances.

**BUSINESS OWNER CHECKLIST**
- Monthly — Close the books. Reconcile accounts, review financial statements, categorize
  outstanding items, and identify unusual activity.
- Quarterly — Review the direction. Look at revenue, expenses, cash, profitability, and major
  changes in the business.
- Annually — Prepare for the next year. Organize tax documents, review systems, evaluate
  recurring costs, and plan the financial priorities ahead.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**
- What is bookkeeping? — Bookkeeping is the ongoing organization and recording of financial
  activity so a business has reliable financial records.
- How often should a business reconcile its accounts? — Frequency depends on transaction volume
  and business needs. MKB's core bookkeeping service is monthly, with biweekly and weekly
  upgrades available.
- What financial statements should a business owner review? — Common core statements include the
  Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow statement.
- Is bookkeeping the same as tax preparation? — No. Bookkeeping organizes financial records
  throughout the year. Tax preparation uses relevant financial and tax information to prepare and
  file a return.
- Is payroll included with bookkeeping? — Payroll processing and payroll tax compliance are
  separate services. Payroll bookkeeping addresses the recording and reconciliation of payroll
  activity.
- Can MKB help if my books are behind? — Yes. Catch-up and historical cleanup are available as
  separate, assessment-based services.
- Can MKB tell me what I should do for my specific tax situation? — MKB can explain the services
  it provides and help organize information. Specific tax advice depends on your circumstances
  and the scope of services engaged.
